How we handle your message

A real reply

Every message gets an answer from a person, usually within two business days. No automated sequence, no drip campaign.

No list, no sharing

Writing to us does not subscribe you to anything. We do not sell, rent, or pass on contact details under any circumstances.

A straight answer

If we do not know something, we say so. If you ask a question we would rather not answer, we will tell you that rather than dodge it.
